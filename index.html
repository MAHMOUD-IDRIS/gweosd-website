Who we are

Building sustainable opportunity through women’s leadership

GWEOSD is a women-led nonprofit organization officially registered in Sudan. It delivers development and entrepreneurship programs for women and men in rural and urban communities affected by conflict, with a focus on recovery, peace and long-term self-reliance.

01

Economic empowerment

Support productive asset ownership, income generation and stronger small and medium enterprises.

02

Women’s leadership

Prepare women leaders with practical skills, confidence, networks and opportunities to lead.

03

Entrepreneurship & innovation

Develop entrepreneurial capabilities, nurture innovation and connect ideas with sustainable pathways.

04

Sustainability & recovery

Promote environmentally responsible practices and support post-war recovery and resilient livelihoods.

Vision

A future shaped by women entrepreneurs

A community led by women entrepreneurs who can create sustainable solutions and build an inclusive future for development.

Mission

Move from assistance to ownership

Empower women and girls affected by war and displacement through productive assets, capacity building, leadership development, innovation and entrepreneurship.

Who we serve

People with ideas, experience and the determination to rebuild

Our target groups include women aged 15–75, women affected by conflict, university graduates, innovators and experienced professionals.
